V3 verification and coverage rules

  • Independent workstreams: government grants/subsidies/insurance/benefits and financial products are tracked separately. Neither workstream is considered complete because a portal link exists.
  • Amount discipline: published assistance is shown with its beneficiary/component conditions. Project-assessed or unpublished amounts are not guessed. A programme allocation is never presented as an individual farmer entitlement.
  • Application status: seasonal and State-specific windows must be checked. Extension-unconfirmed legacy schemes are visible for context but excluded from eligibility matching.
  • Finance discipline: lender product pages and RRB registers do not prove a branch is present in the selected district or that a farmer will qualify for a sanction.
  • Administrative geography: official LGD identifiers are the target source for district, sub-district, village and local-body coverage. The present 779-entry snapshot is explicitly provisional.
  • Last-mile gaps: district crop notifications, local PACS, DCCB, bank branches, BDO, Gram Panchayat and village-level beneficiary targets are not yet comprehensively inventoried.
