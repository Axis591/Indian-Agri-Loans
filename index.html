Data rules used in this directory

  • Amount discipline: published caps are shown as published; bank-assessed limits are marked “need-based”. Unpublished amounts are not guessed.
  • Type discipline: grants, loans, subsidies, insurance and access points are separated.
  • Geography discipline: “National” does not mean every crop or district is currently notified. Seasonal and district notifications control actual availability.
  • Private / informal discipline: regulated lenders are linked to their official product pages. Unlicensed or undocumented lending is not endorsed.
  • Freshness: scheme windows and rates can change. Confirm the current circular with the implementing authority before applying.
