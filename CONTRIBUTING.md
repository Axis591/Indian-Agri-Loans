# Data contribution guide

The goal is a source-backed catalogue, not a list of unsourced scheme names.

## Required research dimensions

For every State/UT, collect data at these levels where available:

1. Central schemes applicable in that State/UT, including the district/crop notification conditions.
2. State Agriculture / Horticulture / Animal Husbandry / Fisheries / Cooperation / Rural Development grants, subsidies and credit programmes.
3. District-specific packages, drought/flood/tribal/hill/command-area programmes and annual calls.
4. Panchayat / block / BDO / PACS / SHG federation / FPO routes and current application windows.
5. Public sector banks, RRBs, cooperative banks, private banks, small finance banks and RBI-regulated NBFC/MFIs.
6. Informal sources only as a risk-information category; never endorse an unlicensed lender or invent terms.

## Minimum fields

Every record must include: scheme/product name, legal provider, `providerKinds` (central/state government, public/private bank, RRB, cooperative/PACS, regulated NBFC/MFI/SFB, local body or informal-risk), type (grant/loan/subsidy/insurance/institution/informal-risk), geographic scope, eligible profiles, exact amount or clearly marked assessment basis, interest/repayment where published, eligibility, step-by-step application path, official application link, evidence URL, last-checked date and material caveats.

## Amount rules

- Use exact published amounts and caps only with a current, authoritative source.
- If the amount varies by crop, acreage, scale of finance, project cost or lender appraisal, say so.
- Separate loan principal from subsidy, guarantee coverage, premium subsidy and amount credited to the farmer.
- Record scheme windows and seasonal crop notifications separately from evergreen scheme eligibility.
- Never convert a proposed budget announcement into a confirmed operational bank limit without a current circular.

## Verification statuses

- `verified`: current primary source confirms the details and geography.
- `assessment`: amount/rate is individually appraised by the lender.
- `not_published`: scheme exists, but public source does not disclose a fixed amount.
- `historical`: source is an older guideline; do not present as currently open until reconfirmed.
- `pending`: research incomplete; do not show as confirmed available.

## Review cadence

Recheck crop insurance and seasonal application windows before each Kharif/Rabi cycle; recheck bank pricing and credit limits at least quarterly; recheck state budgets and department guidelines once each financial year; recheck district/administrative boundaries against LGD monthly.
