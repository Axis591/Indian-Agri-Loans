## Geography and administrative hierarchy

- Official Government of India Local Government Directory catalogue: https://data.gov.in/catalog/local-government-directory-lgd (district, sub-district, village and local-body records; monthly updates are described by OGD).
- Runtime district-list fallback provider: https://github.com/CodingMation/indian-states-districts (community-maintained, not an official government dataset). The dashboard includes a bundled 36-State/UT and 779-district snapshot from this community dataset as a fallback and also attempts to refresh it from the same source at runtime. Cross-check all district names and changes against LGD.

## Important source limitations

- The KCC concessional-interest ceiling changed in the 2025-26 Budget announcement. The dashboard deliberately instructs users to confirm the operative circular and bank sanction terms rather than assuming that the higher ceiling is already available in every branch.
- The PMFBY scheme is notification driven. National scheme eligibility does not prove that a selected crop is insured in a selected district and season.
- West Bengal public scheme summaries do not publish a fixed FSSM subsidy amount or a universal amount for the diversified cropping programme. Those are shown as “amount not published”.
- District-level PACS names, specific bank branches, current block-level beneficiary slots, and informal lender licensing are not comprehensively inventoried in this starter release. The Panchayat entry is a contact/facilitation route only, not a standard funding entitlement.
